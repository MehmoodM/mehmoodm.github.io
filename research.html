MSc Research

Master's Thesis

A Classifier Model for Prostate Cancer Diagnosis Using CNNs and Transfer Learning with Multi-parametric MRI

My MSc thesis investigated the use of convolutional neural networks and transfer learning for prostate cancer classification using multi-parametric magnetic resonance imaging (mpMRI).


The work focused on developing a deep learning framework for automated classification from heterogeneous MRI sequences and formed the basis of my subsequent peer-reviewed journal publication.


Degree: Master of Science in Computer Science
Institution: COMSATS University Islamabad
Period: 2018 – 2021
