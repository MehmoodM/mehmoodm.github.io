Research Background

I hold an MSc in Computer Science from COMSATS University Islamabad, where my thesis focused on prostate cancer classification using convolutional neural networks, transfer learning, and multi-parametric MRI.

My research interests include Medical AI, medical image analysis, computer vision, clinical machine learning, and emerging applications of generative AI in healthcare.
