Research & Publications

Publications

My research work has focused on deep learning, medical image analysis, healthcare applications, and distributed computing. My current research direction is particularly interested in Medical AI and AI-based analysis of medical data.

Featured Research

Medical AI & Prostate Cancer Diagnosis

My MSc research investigated the use of convolutional neural networks and transfer learning for prostate cancer diagnosis using multi-parametric MRI. This work resulted in a first-author journal publication in Frontiers in Oncology.
